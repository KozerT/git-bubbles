👶 Step 1: Projects

Your Title About Projects

A remote project is your online shelf on GitHub. It lets your computer and the cloud talk together so your work is always safe, even if you spill apple juice or drop your tablet!

1. What is a Remote Repository?

When you create toys, drawings, or code blocks on your personal device, they stay inside your physical room. But if you want to share them with playmates or store a safe copy in the starry sky, you create a remote repository.

2. Opening Your Local Toy Box

First, tell your computer folder to begin watching your toys. This turns on Git's gentle snapshot camera. 

# Initializes your empty repository right here on your computer.
git init

3. Connecting the Toy Box to GitHub

Next, we whisper the cloud address to Git so it knows where to deliver snapshots. We call this address origin. 

# Tells Git: "Hey, send my toy snapshots up to this cuddly GitHub link!"
git remote add origin <...... https://github.com/baby/my-toys.git
🍼

Nursery Tip:

Don't be scared by technical jargon!

Why Keep Everything in the Cloud?

Nursery buddies can easily clone your toy box to their screens! They can color in duplicate sketchbooks and suggest new stickers without accidentally scribbling over your favorite crayon drawings.
